# SIA ETF — proposal decision log (internal working draft)

Updated: 2026-11-01
Status: Fibo Agency owner-approved approach; not yet a client agreement.

## Approved response positions

1. **Presentation:** Highlight the bespoke, interactive SIA ETF prototype prepared for the client. Do not present the prototype as a previously delivered commercial project. No other agency cases in the initial reply.
2. **Platform:** Custom coded implementation; no WordPress. Exact application stack and architecture are subject to subsequent technical scoping.
3. **Configurator visuals:** Use pre-produced, photorealistic renders (as in the Architectural Atelier prototype); not real-time interactive 3D. Configuration logic and price recalculation are separate concerns from the renders.
4. **Render production:** Hybrid responsibility model. Client supplies existing reference photos, drawings, 3D assets or similar source material; Fibo Agency prepares additional needed imagery based on the agreed inputs. The number and scope of visuals must be agreed, with visual asset production scoped and priced separately from website/configurator engineering.
5. **Administration and enquiries:** Provide an admin panel for self-service management of the house catalogue, models, size variants, pricing, finish/options, imagery and website content. The website must automatically send customer configuration enquiries to a designated email address. No built-in CRM or enquiry-status dashboard in the initial scope; details such as delivery reliability, consent, spam protection and notification address to be defined during technical specification. The prototype's local-only enquiry download is demonstrative and is not the promised production workflow.

6. **Localization:** The client provides the source descriptions, model details and technical specifications. Fibo Agency prepares LV, EN, LT, ET and RU translations for the client's review and sign-off. Specialist language proofreading, if needed, is scoped and priced separately. The admin panel must allow per-language content edits. Neither language quality nor the technical accuracy of source specifications is assumed approved until client sign-off.

## Open matters

- Details of admin panel permissions, supported edits and enquiry email/anti-spam delivery.
- Catalogue models/options and pricing logic.
- Translation workflow, proofreader choice, translation volume and client approval of all five locales.
- Milestones, delivery schedule, acceptance criteria.
- Separate commercial estimates for the website, configurator, administration, localization, visual production and support.

## Demonstrator limitations

The preview demonstrates interaction and approximate pricing, not approved product specifications or real house prices. The full catalogue, customer data submission/backend, and administration are not yet included in the proof of concept.

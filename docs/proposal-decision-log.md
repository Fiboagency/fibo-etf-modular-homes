Updated: 2026-11-01
Status: Fibo Agency owner-approved approach; not yet a client agreement.

## Approved response positions

1. **Presentation:** Highlight the bespoke, interactive SIA ETF prototype prepared for the client. Do not present the prototype as a previously delivered commercial project. No other agency cases in the initial reply.
2. **Platform:** Custom coded implementation; no WordPress. Exact application stack and architecture are subject to subsequent technical scoping.
3. **Configurator visuals:** Use pre-produced, photorealistic renders (as in the Architectural Atelier prototype); not real-time interactive 3D. Configuration logic and price recalculation are separate concerns from the renders.
4. **Render production:** Hybrid responsibility model. Client supplies existing reference photos, drawings, 3D assets or similar source material; Fibo Agency prepares additional needed imagery based on the agreed inputs. The number and scope of visuals must be agreed, with visual asset production scoped and priced separately from website/configurator engineering.

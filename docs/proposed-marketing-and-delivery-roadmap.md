# ETF Modular Homes — proposed staged delivery & growth roadmap

Updated: 2026-11-01
Status: Internal technical proposal for discussion; not a client-approved scope or delivery commitment.
This public-repository document contains **no rates, prices, commercial conditions or client contact details**.

## Product strategy

Build an adaptable commercial site that supports future marketing and sales, rather than treating the website as an isolated deliverable. The owner-approved delivery approach is a phased MVP followed by optional extensions.

## Suggested delivery phases (subject to a detailed specification)

### Phase 0 — Discovery and architecture
- Agree catalogue schema for at least six house models, dimensions, finish combinations and pricing rules.
- Define accepted source assets, render coverage, content ownership, SEO information architecture and data collection/consent requirements.
- Write acceptance criteria and an implementation plan.

### Phase 1 — Website foundation and catalogue
- Bespoke responsive premium UX/UI, marketing pages, six-model catalogue and model detail templates.
- LV / EN / LT / ET / RU language support with per-language editable content; translations to be reviewed by the client.
- Initial technical SEO: clean URLs, titles, metadata, internal links, performance, crawl/indexing configuration and structured data where appropriate.

### Phase 2 — Configurator, enquiries, administration
- Model-specific sizes and finish/options selection, pre-generated-image switching, price calculation and configuration summary.
- Server-side email enquiries with reliable delivery, spam protection and privacy checks.
- Self-service content and product administration; permissions defined in the specification.

### Phase 3 — Measurement, quality and launch
- Analytics foundation: privacy-aware event measurement, source attribution where available, enquiry funnel dashboards and optional PostHog EU hosting.
- Mobile/browser and accessibility checks, user acceptance, training, launch and monitoring.

## Optional post-launch marketing and sales work

- Paid media readiness: landing page variants, Google Ads and Meta campaign instrumentation, UTM naming conventions, campaign conversion measurement and creative production.
- CRM integration: store qualified leads, follow-up outcomes and lead-to-sale statuses, then feed business results into marketing analysis where permitted.
- Ongoing organic search/content growth, landing page experimentation, monthly reporting and conversion optimization.
- Branding and coordinated online/offline creative as separate scopes when business goals warrant.

## Essential analytics events (proposed)

- Product/model viewed.
- Configurator started and a material configuration changed.
- Configurator completed / quote request initiated.
- Quote request successfully accepted by the server.
- Enquiry qualified, meeting booked and sale closed **only if** CRM workflow is implemented and lawful processing is defined.

## Boundaries and delivery conditions

- Initial work uses pre-generated renders, not interactive 3D.
- Prototype visuals, technical specifications and illustrative prices are not production assets or approved house prices.
- The production enquiry flow is separate from the local file download in the prototype.
- Render creation is a separate workstream based on approved source models and exact variant coverage.
- Marketing media spend, third-party subscriptions and ongoing campaign management are separate from software development.
- Avoid guarantees of search rankings, advertising CPC, lead volumes, launch date or commercial performance.
- Account for analytics consent, data minimization, secure lead handling and privacy notices in the specification.
- Exact schedule and costs to be agreed after technical discovery and scope validation.
